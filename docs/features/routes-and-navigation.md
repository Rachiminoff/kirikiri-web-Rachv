# Routes and Scene Navigation

Rachv provides a route-focused scene list to make it easier to browse the story's indexed scenes.

## Route selector

The route bar provides access to:

- Fate (Saber)
- Unlimited Blade Works (Rin)
- Heaven's Feel (Sakura)

Selecting a route changes the scene list to that route. The same route choices are available in the settings menu.

## Jump to day

The route bar includes day shortcuts. Selecting a day chip scrolls the scene list to the corresponding day section. The labels are localized according to the selected reader language.

## Scene flowchart

The scene list uses a flowchart to represent connections between indexed scenes. Its connectors and arrows help show the direction of the story's scene relationships.

Pointing to or focusing a scene highlights related paths: scenes that follow it and scenes that lead to it receive different highlight colors, while unrelated scenes are visually reduced. This is intended to make the graph easier to read without removing the surrounding context.

## Resume and read markers

The scene list can show the last reading position and mark scenes that have already been opened. The route list can reopen on the route the reader was using and scroll toward the last-read scene.

## Route switching on phones

On touch devices, horizontal swiping can switch between routes. The route bar also remains available for direct selection. Keyboard left/right navigation is supported by the route interface where applicable.

## Important limitation

The flowchart and route progress are based on generated scene-index data. They describe the connections and conditions represented in that data; they do not guarantee that every hidden branch or original-game state has been reproduced.
